Meet the Founder 
Minesh Bhav 
Founder & Tender Management Consultant 
 
Minesh Bhav is a tender management professional with experience in tender bidding, 
government liaison coordination, procurement processes, bid documentation, and 
contract-related activities. 
 
With a practical understanding of tender requirements and procurement procedures, 
Minesh established Minesh Tendering with the objective of helping manufacturers, 
contractors, suppliers, and businesses navigate the tendering process more efficiently. 
 
His approach focuses on identifying relevant opportunities, understanding tender 
eligibility criteria, preparing compliant bid documentation, coordinating with 
stakeholders, and supporting clients throughout the tender lifecycle.
